Free Live CCTV Monitoring for Charities, Community Groups and Not-for-Profit Organisations

At UK Safety Camera Network Ltd (USCN), we believe that organisations providing a public or community benefit should have access to effective security without facing unnecessary financial barriers.

Through our High Watch Not-for-Profit Programme, eligible charities, community groups and other not-for-profit organisations can apply for professional live CCTV monitoring, completely free of charge.

Whether you already have CCTV cameras or need a new system installed, we aim to make accessing our monitoring services as straightforward and affordable as possible.

Who Can Apply?

Our programme is available to eligible not-for-profit organisations within our service areas.

To be considered, your organisation must meet at least one of the following criteria:

Public-facing premises: Your organisation operates from a location where CCTV monitoring could also contribute to the safety of a publicly accessible area, such as a charity shop on a high street.

Direct community benefit: Your organisation provides services or activities that directly benefit the public or a community, even if your premises are not located in a busy public area.

Charitable status alone does not guarantee eligibility. For example, premises used solely for fundraising or routine administration may not qualify unless they also meet the public-facing location requirement.

All applications are assessed individually, taking into account suitability, privacy, security needs and available monitoring capacity.

Already Have CCTV Cameras?

You may be able to connect your existing CCTV system to High Watch without purchasing replacement equipment.

We support a wide range of professional CCTV systems, provided they meet our technical and security requirements. Cameras generally need to be ONVIF-compatible.

Not sure whether your cameras are compatible? That's fine.

Simply contact us with the manufacturer and model of your existing cameras or recording system, and we'll help you establish whether they can be connected.

Don't Have CCTV? We May Be Able to Help

If your organisation doesn't already have suitable security cameras, we can explore the options available to you.

Option 1: Free USCN-Funded Cameras
Where funding and resources permit, USCN may be able to supply and install CCTV cameras completely free of charge.

These cameras remain the property of UK Safety Camera Network Ltd. We take responsibility for their ongoing operation, maintenance, security updates and replacement when required.

Option 2: Purchase Compatible Cameras
If fully funded equipment isn't available, you can still apply for free live monitoring by purchasing suitable cameras.

You can purchase equipment through USCN, potentially benefiting from discounted hardware or installation charges, or arrange installation through an independent CCTV installer.

You do not have to use USCN's installation services to participate. However, all equipment must meet our compatibility, security and operational requirements before being accepted.

In some circumstances, we may also be able to offer a combination of funded and customer-purchased equipment.

Free Live Monitoring

Once accepted and connected, your approved CCTV cameras can be monitored by our human operators during USCN Live's operational hours.

Our operators observe camera feeds, assess potential security incidents and, where appropriate, contact nominated representatives or emergency services.

There is no charge for the live monitoring service under the High Watch Not-for-Profit Programme.

Monitoring is provided free of charge during our published operating hours and does not guarantee that every incident will be detected or prevented.

6PM - 1AM Mon-Sun

We Take Care of CCTV Data Protection

Under the High Watch Not-for-Profit Programme, USCN is responsible for determining and managing the CCTV processing arrangements for cameras accepted into the programme, acting as the data controller where established by those arrangements.

This includes managing CCTV recordings, handling data subject access requests, setting appropriate retention periods and applying relevant data protection safeguards.

Our aim is to reduce the administrative burden on participating organisations so they can concentrate on delivering their services.

Where existing cameras are integrated, responsibilities and access arrangements are established as part of onboarding.

How Is the Programme Funded?

High Watch is supported by resources from USCN's commercial activities and external funding, including grants where available.

This allows us to direct resources towards organisations delivering a public or community benefit, while balancing the costs of equipment, maintenance and professional monitoring.

Apply for High Watch NFP

If your charity, community group or not-for-profit organisation could benefit from free live CCTV monitoring, we'd welcome an application.

Whether you have an existing CCTV system or need help obtaining suitable equipment, contact our team to discuss your circumstances.

Please note: High Watch is a discretionary programme. Applications are subject to eligibility, service-area coverage, technical suitability, privacy assessments, funding and available monitoring capacity. Free equipment and installation are not guaranteed, and any applicable hardware or installation charges will be explained before you proceed.

Contact USCN Live to discuss your organisation's eligibility and available options.
