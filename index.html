About Us

What is High Watch Community?
High Watch is a community safety initiative operated by USCN Live, combining our free community camera hosting programme with professional live CCTV monitoring.

Through High Watch Community Programme, eligible businesses can host a USCN-owned CCTV camera at no installation or maintenance cost. In return, we ask for a suitable mounting location and access to a small amount of electricity and internet connectivity.

Unlike our standard Host a Camera programme, High Watch Community Programme cameras can also be monitored by our control room operators during USCN Live's operational hours, helping us identify potential incidents and contact the appropriate people or emergency services when necessary.

How does it work?
USCN installs, owns and maintains the camera, including software updates and equipment replacement when required.

Our operators can view the live camera feed during monitoring hours, while recordings are managed by USCN in accordance with our applicable data protection procedures.

Can my business join?
Businesses within our service areas are welcome to apply for High Watch Community Programme.

However, acceptance is not guaranteed. Each application is assessed individually, taking into account community safety needs, privacy considerations, location suitability and available monitoring capacity.

If we're unable to offer High Watch Community Programme at your location, we may be able to offer an alternative through our standard Host a Camera programme.

This provides many of the same benefits, including free camera installation and ongoing maintenance, but without routine live monitoring.

Supporting Safer Communities
High Watch Community Programme is part of our commitment to making community safety technology more accessible.

By combining community camera infrastructure with the capabilities of our USCN Live control room, we aim to provide additional protection in locations where active monitoring can offer a meaningful public-safety benefit.
