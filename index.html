Unlike USCN Live, which monitors existing cameras at one business, High Watch is a dedicated area-wide network. We install Axis PTZ and fixed cameras to support overlapping views and incident tracking between locations.

SHARED INVESTMENT

Share the installation. Share the protection.

High Watch is a shared network. Two or more participating shops can spread the cost of specialist cameras and installation, while Business Improvement Districts can fund wider-area coverage.

We assess the area, design the camera network and provide an installation proposal.
