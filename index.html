Making Communities Safer

Through free CCTV installation, ongoing maintenance and professional live monitoring, USCN High Watch helps protect high streets, public spaces and local communities. We work with businesses and property owners to provide community safety cameras in locations where they can make a meaningful difference.

What is High Watch Community?

High Watch is a community safety initiative operated by USCN Live, combining our free community camera hosting programme with professional live CCTV monitoring.

Through High Watch Community Programme, eligible businesses can host a USCN-owned CCTV camera at no installation or maintenance cost. In return, we ask for a suitable mounting location and access to a small amount of electricity and internet connectivity.

Unlike our standard Host a Camera programme, High Watch Community Programme cameras can also be monitored by our control room operators during USCN Live's operational hours, helping us identify potential incidents and contact the appropriate people or emergency services when necessary.

How does it work?

USCN installs, owns and maintains the camera, including software updates and equipment replacement when required.

Our operators can view the live camera feed during monitoring hours, while recordings are managed by USCN in accordance with our applicable data protection procedures.

Can my business join?

Businesses within our service areas are welcome to apply for High Watch Community Programme.

However, acceptance is not guaranteed. Each application is assessed individually, taking into account community safety needs, privacy considerations, location suitability and available monitoring capacity.

If we're unable to offer High Watch Community Programme at your location, we may be able to offer an alternative through our standard Host a Camera programme.

This provides many of the same benefits, including free camera installation and ongoing maintenance, but without routine live monitoring.

Your Questions About High Watch

Find out how USCN High Watch works, who can apply and how our free community CCTV cameras and professional live monitoring help support safer public spaces.

What is USCN High Watch?

USCN High Watch is a free community safety CCTV programme operated by UK Safety Camera Network Ltd. We provide, install, own and maintain CCTV cameras at approved locations, helping monitor high streets and public spaces. Our control room operators can view live footage during operating hours, identify potential incidents and coordinate appropriate responses. The programme focuses on wider community safety rather than dedicated security for individual businesses.

Who can apply for USCN High Watch?

Businesses, property owners and other suitable premises within our service areas can apply to host a High Watch camera. Each application is assessed individually, considering the location, potential community safety benefits, privacy requirements and available resources. Acceptance is not guaranteed.

Is USCN High Watch completely free?

Yes. Approved High Watch hosts receive CCTV equipment, installation, maintenance and live monitoring free of charge. The cameras remain the property of UK Safety Camera Network Ltd. Hosts may be required to provide a suitable mounting location, electricity and internet connectivity under the hosting agreement.

Who owns and maintains the CCTV cameras?

All High Watch cameras are owned and operated by UK Safety Camera Network Ltd. We are responsible for their maintenance, software updates, security and replacement when required. Participating businesses do not need to purchase or independently maintain the camera equipment.

Are High Watch cameras monitored 24 hours a day?

No. High Watch cameras are monitored by our human operators during USCN Live's published operating hours. Outside these hours, cameras may continue recording in accordance with their configured retention and recording arrangements. Live monitoring does not guarantee that every incident will be detected or prevented.

Can High Watch monitor my existing CCTV cameras?

No. High Watch is designed around community safety cameras supplied, owned and operated by USCN. If you already have CCTV cameras and would like professional live monitoring, our standard paid USCN Live service may be suitable. Eligible public bodies, charities and not-for-profit organisations may also be able to apply through our separate USCN Live Smartwatch programme.

What happens if your operators detect suspicious activity?

If our operators identify a potential incident, they assess the situation and take appropriate action. Depending on the circumstances, this may include contacting the police, emergency services or other relevant contacts. Our aim is to support timely responses to incidents affecting public and community safety.

What happens if my High Watch application is unsuccessful?

Not every location can be accepted into High Watch. If we're unable to provide live monitoring, we may consider your location for our standard Host a Camera programme, which provides free USCN-owned community CCTV without routine live monitoring. Any alternative remains subject to eligibility, location suitability and available resources.
