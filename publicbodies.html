High Watch for Public Bodies

Free Live CCTV Monitoring for Councils, Public Authorities and Public-Sector Organisations

High Watch for Public Bodies is a programme operated by UK Safety Camera Network Ltd (USCN), offering eligible public bodies access to professional live CCTV monitoring completely free of charge.

Whether you're a local council looking to reduce control-room costs, a transport authority seeking additional monitoring coverage, or a public organisation looking to improve safety in a particular area, High Watch may be able to help.

Our approach is flexible. You can connect existing CCTV cameras, apply for USCN-funded equipment, install new cameras independently, or combine these options to create a solution suited to your organisation.

Already Have CCTV Cameras?

If your organisation already operates CCTV cameras, you may be able to connect them to our monitoring network.

Our control room operators can monitor approved cameras, identify potential incidents and coordinate appropriate responses with police, emergency services, your existing control room or other nominated contacts.

Where authorised and appropriate, operators can also use multiple camera feeds to follow developing incidents and provide relevant information to responding authorities.

There is no charge for live monitoring of cameras accepted into the High Watch programme.

We assess each application individually and may be able to monitor all or only some of your existing cameras, depending on operational capacity and suitability.

Already Have CCTV Cameras?

You may be able to connect your existing CCTV system to High Watch without purchasing replacement equipment.

We support a wide range of professional CCTV systems, provided they meet our technical and security requirements. Cameras generally need to be ONVIF-compatible.

Not sure whether your cameras are compatible? That's fine.

Simply contact us with the manufacturer and model of your existing cameras or recording system, and we'll help you establish whether they can be connected.

Need New CCTV Cameras?

We can also help public bodies establish additional CCTV coverage.

Option 1: USCN-Funded Cameras

Where funding and resources permit, we may be able to supply and install CCTV cameras free of charge.

These cameras remain the property of UK Safety Camera Network Ltd. We take responsibility for their operation, ongoing maintenance, security updates and replacement when required.

Your organisation will normally be responsible for obtaining the necessary permissions and arranging or funding supporting infrastructure, including suitable mounting locations, electricity, network connectivity and any required planning or third-party works.

Option 2: Public-Body-Funded Cameras

If we cannot fund a proposed installation, your organisation can still benefit from free live monitoring by funding the required equipment and installation.

You can arrange installation through USCN or use an independent CCTV installer.

Provided the equipment meets our technical, security and operational requirements, it can be assessed for connection to the High Watch monitoring network.

Option 3: Combine Your Options

You don't have to choose just one approach.

For example, a council could connect several existing cameras, receive additional USCN-funded cameras and purchase further cameras where necessary.

All cameras accepted into High Watch receive free live monitoring, regardless of which approved equipment route is used.

Professional Monitoring and Incident Response

High Watch cameras are monitored by human operators during USCN Live's operational hours.

Our operators can identify suspicious activity, assess developing incidents and communicate relevant information to designated contacts or emergency services where appropriate.

Monitoring is not restricted to one geographical area, although camera access and monitoring arrangements must be individually approved.

High Watch does not guarantee that every incident will be detected or prevented.

An Alternative: Host a Camera

If your organisation needs additional CCTV infrastructure but does not require live monitoring, our standard Host a Camera programme may be suitable.

Public bodies can apply to host USCN-owned community safety cameras, supplied and maintained free of charge where approved and available.

Unlike High Watch, these cameras are not routinely monitored live. They provide recorded footage that can be retrieved and used when appropriate.

What Does It Cost?

Live monitoring is completely free for cameras accepted into High Watch for Public Bodies.

Existing camera integration, new equipment and installation arrangements are assessed individually.

Where USCN cannot fund an installation, your organisation may need to cover equipment, installation labour, electricity, connectivity, permissions or other supporting infrastructure costs.

Any applicable charges will be explained before work is agreed.

Apply for High Watch

High Watch for Public Bodies is open to applications from eligible councils, public authorities, transport organisations, emergency services, healthcare bodies and other public-sector organisations.

Every application is reviewed individually, taking into account public-safety needs, technical compatibility, privacy, legal requirements, available funding and monitoring capacity.

Acceptance is not guaranteed, and we may offer monitoring for only a selection of the cameras requested.

Contact UK Safety Camera Network Ltd to discuss your existing CCTV infrastructure, proposed locations and the support available through High Watch.
