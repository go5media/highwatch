The High Watch approach

Security designed around an area, not just one camera.

A single shop's CCTV may show what happened inside its premises. A connected High Watch scheme can bring together additional views from participating locations, helping operators understand an incident as it moves through a covered area.

Connect existing cameras

Where technically suitable and properly authorised, relevant feeds from a business's existing CCTV installation can be incorporated into the monitoring arrangements.

Install High Watch cameras

We can design and install dedicated cameras inside premises, outside shopfronts and at other appropriate locations to address identified coverage needs.

Coordinate observations

During agreed monitoring hours, operators can use available views to observe relevant activity across participating locations and follow the development of an incident where coverage permits.

Coverage and camera design

Coverage planned for the places that matter.

High Watch is not simply a fixed number of cameras per shop. Camera placement is assessed in the context of the whole area, with attention to entrances, approaches, relevant shared spaces and gaps between viewpoints.

Indoor, external and area-wide views

A scheme may combine indoor shop cameras, shopfront cameras and, where lawful permissions and practical conditions allow, cameras at other suitable positions such as poles or street-lighting infrastructure.

PTZ cameras and planned tours

Pan-tilt-zoom cameras may be operated manually or configured with appropriate preset tours. Designs consider what a moving camera stops seeing and whether another view can cover that area. Continuous coverage is not assumed.

What a connected scheme might include

For a small commercial parade or BID area:

  • Participating shops with existing CCTV feeds
  • New High Watch cameras where coverage is needed
  • Exterior views of approaches and entrances
  • Additional area cameras where justified and authorised
  • Agreed monitoring and incident-escalation procedures
  • Recorded footage from cameras configured for recording, subject to applicable retention and access arrangements

The diagram and examples describe possible configurations, not a promise that every location will have uninterrupted visibility or that every incident will be detected.

Governance and responsibilities

Clear responsibility for different camera systems.

UK Safety Camera Network Ltd is intended to act as the data controller for cameras it operates as part of the High Watch programme, subject to the actual arrangements and applicable law. Existing CCTV systems operated by participating businesses may have different controllers and responsibilities.

High Watch-operated cameras

USCN would manage its own applicable controller obligations, including relevant data protection requests, subject to the final contractual and operational arrangements. This does not automatically remove all responsibilities from participating businesses.

Existing business CCTV

Connecting an existing system does not, by itself, transfer its legal responsibilities to USCN. The controller and processor roles, permitted access, sharing and retention arrangements must be established for each integration.

Camera coverage, monitoring, recording, disclosure and information sharing must be necessary, proportionate and lawful. Access to footage and responses to data protection requests are handled according to the responsibilities established for each system.
Frequently asked questions

Questions about High Watch

Is High Watch only for shops?

No. It is designed for individual businesses, groups of nearby premises, commercial areas and Business Improvement Districts where coordinated camera coverage would be appropriate.

Can we keep our existing CCTV system?

Potentially. Existing cameras may be integrated where technically compatible, suitably configured and authorised. High Watch cameras can also be installed to address gaps in coverage.

What hours are actively monitored?

The described High Watch service has a scheduled live monitoring window from 6 pm to 1 am. Recording outside those hours depends on the configuration, lawful basis, retention arrangements and operational setup of each camera system.

Can operators follow a person between shops?

Where there is a legitimate incident-related purpose and suitable connected coverage, operators may use different camera views to follow relevant activity. This is not a guarantee of uninterrupted tracking, and monitoring must comply with applicable law and operating procedures.

Are the cameras always recording?

Recording arrangements depend on the camera configuration and the approved scheme. Live monitoring hours do not necessarily determine recording hours. Recording coverage and retention must be confirmed for each deployment.

Who handles data protection requests?

Responsibilities depend on who controls each camera system and the agreed data-sharing arrangements. USCN is intended to manage controller obligations for its own High Watch-operated cameras; existing business systems may remain under their own controllers.

How much does High Watch cost?

For BID-funded networks, monitoring is £149.99 per participating shop per month plus £49.99 per additional outdoor camera per month. Either service can be selected independently. Private business networks are £199.99 per participating shop per month. Installation and maintenance are quoted separately.
